# AGENTS.md — Agent Etiquette & Operating Rules for Dr. Praduman Khachar Portfolio

## 🏛️ Project Mandate
This repository hosts the official portfolio, academic archive, and digital library for **Dr. Pradumankumar B. Khachar** (Historian, Author of 33 books, Associate Professor, YouTuber, INTACH Convener).

The goal is to maintain an **uncompromisingly high-taste, high-extractability, search-and-AI-friendly digital platform** (AEO/SEO optimized) while keeping identity markers strictly verified.

---

## 🎨 Design & Craft Directives
1. **Design System & Taste:**
   - Follow the **Refero Design** and **Design Taste Frontend** methodologies.
   - Aesthetic tone: Editorial, scholarly, trustworthy, parchment/warm ink tones (`#2c2418` dark chrome, earthy terracotta, warm ivory canvas).
   - Typography: Clean serif / Sans pairing, high contrast, readable line lengths.
   - Avoid generic AI slop: no loud unneeded neon gradients, no random decorative serif word-swaps without semantic meaning.

2. **Entity Consistency & AEO (Answer Engine Optimization):**
   - Canonical Entity Name: `Dr. Pradumankumar B. Khachar` (also matching `Dr. Praduman Khachar` & `ડૉ. પ્રદ્યુમ્ન ખાચર`).
   - Every major claim (33 books, 23 in Library of Congress, INTACH Convener, BAOU syllabus author, BBC citations) must be corroborated in JSON-LD Schema markup and `llms.txt`.
   - Keep page content concise and direct at the top (40–60 word answer blocks first).

---

## 🤖 Agent Interaction Etiquette
- **Autopilot:** Continue towards completion without asking redundant questions unless explicitly pivoting, deleting data, or asking for non-recoverable operations.
- **Verification:** Execute `npm run build` or lint checks before claiming work complete.
- **No Reverts:** Do not revert user changes or existing commits unless instructed.
- **Commit Messages:** Use clear, conventional commit messages (`feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`, `polish:`).
