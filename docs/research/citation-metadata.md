# Citation metadata notes

Each catalog record now has contributor names and roles. Most works credit
Pradumankumar B. Khachar as author. Exceptions are kept explicitly in the data:

- **Tasviroma Junagadh:** Khachar and Dhirubhai Vala, from the existing catalog
  description. Do not infer an unrecorded middle initial.
- **Kathio No Itihas / History of Kathi (2019):** John W. Watson as author,
  Khachar as editor. The existing About page records the editorial role, and the
  [2019 publication report](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/rajkot/watsons-19th-century-manuscript-on-kathis-and-kathiavad-released-today/articleshow/70719041.cms)
  identifies Watson and confirms Khachar edited the Gujarati and English editions.
- **Kathiyavad Sarvasangrah:** Watson as author, based on its existing title and
  description. Khachar's precise role is not confirmed in the available metadata,
  so an additional contributor role is not inferred.

RIS uses repeated AU fields for authors, A3 for book editors, and A4 for
translators, following [Zotero's RIS mapping](https://github.com/zotero/translators/blob/master/RIS.js).
ISBNs are exported as SN only when the ISBN-10/ISBN-13 length and checksum pass.
Five existing ISBN strings fail validation and remain untouched in the catalog;
they are omitted from citation exports pending a cover/catalog check. Passing a
checksum establishes syntax, not independent verification of ownership.
