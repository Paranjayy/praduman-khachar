## How to Add Self-Published Books to Google Books

### Option 1: Google Books Partner Program
- **URL:** https://books.google.com/partners/
- **Requirements:** ISBN required for most submissions
- **Process:** Upload PDF, add metadata, Google reviews and publishes
- **Cost:** Free

### Option 2: Via Publisher (if books have a publisher)
- Contact the publisher (Pravin Prakashan, Gujarat Sahitya Akademi, etc.)
- Ask them to submit to Google Books through their partner account
- They control the listing

### Option 3: ISBN Assignment for Self-Published Books
- **Indian ISBN Agency:** Raja Rammohun Roy National Agency for ISBN
  - **URL:** https://www.nisbn.org.in/
  - **Fee:** Free for Indian publishers
  - **Process:** Register as publisher, apply for ISBN block, assign to each book
  - **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks

### Option 4: Without ISBN (Limited)
- Google Books **requires** ISBN for most listings
- Alternative: Upload to **Archive.org** (no ISBN needed)
- Archive.org is already being used for some of Dr. Khachar's books

### Option 5: Create ISBNs Retrospectively
- Register as a publisher with the Indian ISBN agency
- Get a block of ISBNs (free)
- Assign ISBNs to existing books
- Update listings on Amazon, Flipkart, etc.
- Submit to Google Books
